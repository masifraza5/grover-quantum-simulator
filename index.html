How the Algorithm Works

1 · Initialize Start in the initial state |0...0⟩.
2 · Hadamard Create an equal superposition of all possible states.
3 · Oracle Flip the sign of the marked state's amplitude.
4 · Diffusion Invert amplitudes about their mean to amplify the marked state.
5 · Repeat Repeat the oracle and diffusion operations for each Grover iteration.
